# Arci Client Portal — Content Guide

Tone: friendly, clear, professional but not stiff. Like talking to a knowledgeable teammate.
Language: English

## 1. Login Page

**Page title:** Welcome back

**Subheading:** Sign in to your Arci client portal

**Fields:**
- Email address
- Password

**Button:** Sign in

**Forgot password link:** Forgot your password?

**Reset password page heading:** Reset your password
**Reset subtext:** Enter your email and we'll send you a link to get back in.
**Reset button:** Send reset link

**Error messages:**
- Wrong credentials: "Hmm, that doesn't look right. Double-check your email and password."
- Account not found: "We couldn't find an account with that email."
- Too many attempts: "Too many attempts. Please wait a moment and try again."
